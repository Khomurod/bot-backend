# Credits: the celebration car

`wenze-car.glb` is derived from **"Chevrolet Malibu Premier 2022"** by
**Type_S** (<https://sketchfab.com/Type_S>), published on Sketchfab
(<https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/chevrolet-malibu-premier-2022-55aa80115ee04aa79c802f3baf454326>)
and licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).
The download came through a re-upload by Glory Hunter; the file's own metadata
names Type_S as the author and the page above as the source, so the credit is
Type_S's.

**Changes made here (26 October 2026):**

- the second set of right-hand wheels, which sat exactly over the first, is
  removed;
- the wheels are renamed `WheelFrontLeft`, `WheelRearRight`, … with their rims
  `…Rim`, which is what the scene's contact shadows look for;
- WENZE licence plates are added at the front (the lower grille) and the back
  (the trunk recess) — the camera only ever sees the front;
- geometry is quantized and meshopt-compressed (6.9 MB → 0.85 MB).

The paint, the Chevrolet badges and the MALIBU lettering are the model's own,
kept at the owner's request (26 October 2026). The CC BY licence covers the
model, not GM's trademarks.

The attribution is shown on screen while the car is (`CelebrationOverlay.jsx`).
It replaced Khronos' "Car Concept" (Eric Chadwick, Darmstadt Graphics Group),
which is in git history before this date.
